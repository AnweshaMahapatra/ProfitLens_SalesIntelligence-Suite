# Business Questions & Insights

## *Stakeholder Review Analysis Sales Insight Dashboard*

*10 strategic business questions a leadership team, stakeholder, or hiring manager would naturally ask while reviewing the dashboard, answered through data-driven analysis and business interpretation.*

# 📌 Q1. Delhi NCR generates over half of our revenue. Is that a strength or a hidden business risk?

### **Answer: It looks like strength on the surface, but operationally, it is a major concentration risk.**

Delhi NCR contributes:

* **₹520M Revenue**
* **52.76% of total business revenue**
* **48.5% of total profit contribution**

At first glance, this market appears to be the backbone of the company.

But there’s a problem hidden underneath the scale.

Despite generating more than half the business revenue, Delhi NCR operates at only:

# ⚠️ 2.3% Profit Margin

That means the organization is heavily dependent on a market that delivers volume without delivering strong profitability.

The real danger is structural dependency.

If Delhi NCR experiences:

* demand slowdown,
* operational disruption,
* competitive pressure,
* or customer loss,

The impact would immediately affect the entire organization.

A healthy revenue mix rarely allows one market to exceed 25–30% contribution.

The business should strategically use Delhi NCR’s cash flow to strengthen secondary markets like:

* Mumbai
* Ahmedabad
* Bhopal

before the dependency becomes irreversible.

# 📌 Q2. Our top customer contributes nearly 42% of revenue. Should leadership be worried?

### **Answer: Absolutely — this is one of the biggest strategic risks visible in the dashboard.**

Electricalsara Stores contributes:

* **₹413M Revenue**
* **41.97% of total revenue**

That means:

# ⚠️ Nearly half the company depends on a single customer.

However, despite its massive scale, the account operates at only:

# 📉 2.3% Profit Margin

Now compare that with smaller customers:

| Customer           | Profit Margin % |
| ------------------ | --------------- |
| Leader             | 7.5%            |
| Nixon              | 4.1%            |
| Electricalslytical | 3.4%            |

These smaller accounts are significantly more efficient.

This creates a dangerous imbalance:

* The company prioritizes volume,
* while profitability remains concentrated elsewhere.

If Electricalsara reduces orders, renegotiates aggressively, or exits entirely, the financial shock would be severe.

This is not just customer concentration.
It is a business dependency.

# 📌 Q3. Which markets are actively damaging profitability?

### **Answer: Bengaluru is the clearest operational red flag in the entire business.**

Bengaluru records:

# ❌ –20.8% Profit Margin

This means:

> The company loses money on every rupee generated in Bengaluru.

Even more concerning:

* Bengaluru contributes only **0.04% of total revenue**

So the business is:

* losing money,
* at a very low scale.

That combination is operationally indefensible.

Kanpur also shows:

* **–0.5% margin**

But Bengaluru requires immediate intervention.

At this stage, leadership should ask:

* Are logistics costs too high?
* Is pricing incorrect?
* Is customer acquisition inefficient?
* Are operational costs exceeding recovery?

Without rapid correction, Bengaluru should be restructured or strategically exited.

# 📌 Q4. What is the revenue trend actually telling us about business health?

### **Answer: The trend suggests the business peaked in 2018 and has been steadily weakening since then.**

The Revenue Trend chart shows monthly revenue approaching:

# 📈 ₹40M/month in mid-2018

However, by early 2020:

* monthly revenue declined to nearly:

# 📉 ₹10M–₹15M/month

That represents:

# ⚠️ More than 60% decline from peak performance

This is not a temporary fluctuation.

The decline continues consistently across multiple quarters, indicating:

* weakening market demand,
* competitive pressure,
* channel inefficiency,
* or customer erosion.

The 2020 analysis strengthens this concern:

* Revenue: **₹142M**
* Profit Margin: **₹2.1M**
* Margin % fluctuating dangerously close to zero

Even more concerning:

# ❌ No month in 2020 outperformed the prior year equivalent.

The dashboard acts less like a reporting tool here
and more like an early-warning system.

The dashboard cannot fully answer this yet
